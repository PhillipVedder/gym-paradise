# Asset provenance

All equipment is original procedural Babylon.js geometry created for this prototype. No third-party equipment assets are included.

`public/art/paradise-mural.png` was created using the built-in image generator for this project. Original concept artwork, not a supplied gym photograph.

Exact prompt:

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: one flat raster wall mural texture for Gym Paradise, a 3D gym builder.
Primary request: Create exactly one original wide 3:1 mural artwork, filled edge to edge with dynamic muscular adult anime/comic athletes in training poses and confident intense expressions. Distribute the subjects across the entire width, with bold readable silhouettes and large expressive faces.
Style/medium: original hand-inked modern comic mural, strong graphite and black ink outlines, angular muscular anatomy, energetic motion strokes, screenprint-like graphic shading.
Composition/framing: flat orthographic artwork only, wide horizontal 3:1 composition; seamless-looking dark abstract background at left and right edges; artwork will cover a large flat wall above a mirror inside a dark industrial private gym. The image itself must show only the mural artwork, never the room or mirror.
Color palette: deep purple and violet with cyan accents, charcoal black shadows, restrained white and pink highlights.
Lighting/mood: powerful, focused, energetic, visually readable at distance.
Constraints: original characters only; no existing franchise characters; no text, lettering, logos, signatures, watermarks, UI, mockup, room perspective, gym scene, wall border, or frame. The deliverable is one finished raster artwork, not variations.
