# Asset provenance

All equipment is original procedural Babylon.js geometry created for this prototype. No third-party equipment assets are included.

`public/art/paradise-mural.png` was created using the built-in image generator for this project. Original concept artwork, not a supplied gym photograph.

Exact prompt:

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: one flat raster wall mural texture for Gym Paradise, a 3D gym builder.
Primary request: Create exactly one original wide 3:1 mural artwork, filled edge to edge with dynamic muscular adult anime/comic athletes in training poses and confident intense expressions. Distribute the subjects across the entire width, with bold readable silhouettes and large expressive faces.
Style/medium: original hand-inked modern comic mural, strong graphite and black ink outlines, angular muscular anatomy, energetic motion strokes, screenprint-like graphic shading.
Composition/framing: flat orthographic artwork only, wide horizontal 3:1 composition; seamless-looking dark abstract background at left and right edges; artwork will cover a large flat wall above a mirror inside a dark industrial private gym. The image itself must show only the mural artwork, never the room or mirror.
Color palette: deep purple and violet with cyan accents, charcoal black shadows, restrained white and pink highlights.
Lighting/mood: powerful, focused, energetic, visually readable at distance.
Constraints: original characters only; no existing franchise characters; no text, lettering, logos, signatures, watermarks, UI, mockup, room perspective, gym scene, wall border, or frame. The deliverable is one finished raster artwork, not variations.

## User-provided Sketchfab reference — checked 6 September 2026

Profile: [Mike · 3D Muscle Model](https://sketchfab.com/mikeshortall1991).

The public pages were intermittently blocked (403); indexed creator pages and the public model metadata API were available. Both candidates below returned `isDownloadable: false` and an empty `license` object from `https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/{uid}`. No geometry was downloaded, no internal viewer assets were extracted, and no purchase or contact was made.

- [Hip Thrust Glute Drive Machine](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hip-thrust-glute-drive-machine-plate-loaded-24a2f90f299c456b8668d4b0b0855fb9): 13,186 triangles, 6,917 vertices. Creator describes a standalone plate-loaded gym machine. Strong candidate if a suitable download and reuse licence become available.
- [Dual Functional Trainer](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dual-functional-trainer-gym-fitness-machine-21bc1760bef745f7b4b966727a3733e6): 80,228 triangles, 40,265 vertices. Creator says it is inspired by Prime's functional trainer. Requires a mobile asset/performance review as well as a licence and downloadable file.

The profile also includes high-polygon anatomical exercise animations. Those are distinct from lightweight standalone equipment assets. The selected examples carry NoAI tags on their public pages; no asset from this profile is used for generation or model training. The in-app Research tab links out to the two candidates and states their availability accurately.

New boxing ring, heavy bag station, jump boxes and storage geometry are original procedural approximations. New wall textures and room structures are generated by original drawing/mesh code; the user's gym photos serve as visual references and are not redistributed as application assets.
